# Opterra voice — the human source of truth

Machine-enforceable subset lives in `config/voice.toml`. If you change a rule
here, change it there too, or the validator will not catch it.

## The register

Every line should sound like it came from a calm, intelligent partner — not a
software company running a promotion. After reading an Opterra ad, a practice
owner should feel that things could be *simpler*, not that they are behind.

## Non-negotiable

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Lowercase throughout** — headlines, body, display paths, CTAs | The visual signature of the brand. Proper nouns keep their capitals (see the allow-list in `config/voice.toml`). |
| **No fear-based language** | No "you're losing money", "don't miss out", "before it's too late". Opterra never sells on loss. |
| **No corporate or clinical tone** | No "cutting-edge AI solution", no "streamline your workflow". |
| **No emoji** | On any platform, in any field. |
| **Complete sentences** | Fragments-as-style are off-voice. |
| **Never "healer"** | Use "wellness business owner", "practice owner", or "you". |
| **Outcome-led** | Lead with what changes for the practitioner, not what the product does. |
| **Second person** | "you", "your practice". |
| **Anti-overwhelm** | Everything reads simpler after, not more urgent. |

## The test

Read the line aloud. If it sounds like a vendor pitching, rewrite it. If it
sounds like someone who has actually sat at a front desk at 4pm with two
patients waiting and the phone ringing, keep it.
